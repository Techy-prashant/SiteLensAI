# OSHA Fall Protection Standards — 29 CFR 1926 Subpart M

## General Requirements (1926.501)

Each employee on a walking/working surface with an unprotected side or edge which is 6 feet (1.8 m) or more above a lower level shall be protected from falling by the use of guardrail systems, safety net systems, or personal fall arrest systems.

**Guardrail Exemption**: When standard guardrails, perimeter barriers, or parapet walls are present, workers are fully permitted to stand, walk, and work near the edge. Personal fall arrest harnesses are NOT required when guardrail systems are in place.

Standing or walking near an edge protected by guardrails is SAFE and COMPLIANT. It should be logged as a standard observation or low-priority notice (INFO or WARNING at most), NEVER as a CRITICAL emergency alert.

## Fall Protection Systems (1926.502)

### Guardrail Systems (Primary Protection)
- Top edge height: 42 inches (±3 inches) above walking/working level
- Midrails installed at a height midway between top edge and walking/working surface
- When guardrails are installed, workers may freely work near the perimeter without additional harness lanyards.

### Personal Fall Arrest Systems (For Unguarded Edges Only)
- Full body harness connected to anchorage point required ONLY on edges where no guardrail or barrier exists.
- Anchorage must support at least 5,000 pounds per worker
- Harness must be inspected before each use

### Safety Net Systems
- Installed as close as practicable under the walking/working surface when guardrails are unfeasible.

## Hazard Classification Criteria for Edges

- **SAFE / INFO**: Worker standing or working near an edge where guardrails, safety netting, or perimeter railings are installed. No harness needed.
- **WARNING / ALERT**: Worker near an edge where guardrails are partially installed or undergoing setup, or minor perimeter observations. Alert the worker gently.
- **CRITICAL**: Worker actively leaning over an UNGUARDED edge (no railing, no barrier) at height over 6 feet without any harness, or an active fall in progress.

## Emergency Response

In the event of a fall:
1. Activate emergency response immediately (call 911)
2. Do NOT move the fallen worker unless there is immediate danger
3. Provide first aid while waiting for emergency services
4. Secure the area to prevent additional falls
